## Flag 1 Model Details

**Training**:
- Features: g, BP, RP, BP-RP (Gaia photometry only)
- Target: Teff with polynomial correction (T>10000K)
- Training data: 711,666 flag 1 sources (highest quality)
- Algorithm: Random Forest (300 trees)

**Performance**:
- Test MAE: 195 K
- Test R²: 0.9126
- Mean RF uncertainty: 265 K (full tree method)

**Key advantage**: Training exclusively on flag 1 sources creates
a model specialized for high-quality photometry, complementing
the other three models which use all available training data.
